# Complaint Center — English Guide

This guide can be copied into a Discord channel or used as the English entry panel description.

## What is the Complaint Center?

The Complaint Center lets you report a problem or share feedback with the community moderation team in private. Each complaint creates its own private channel, visible only to you and the team that handles that type of complaint.

## How to submit a complaint

1. Go to the channel with the **Complaint Center** panel.
2. Click **Submit a Complaint**.
3. Choose the complaint type that best matches your situation. The type details show which team will see your ticket.
4. Fill out the form. Fields marked as required must be completed.
5. Review the confirmation page and click **Submit**.
6. The bot creates a private channel for you. You will receive a link to it.

## What happens after you submit

- A private ticket channel is created. Only you and the selected handling team can see it.
- The handling team is notified.
- You can talk with the team directly in that channel, upload evidence, and answer follow-up questions.
- When the issue is resolved, an administrator can archive the ticket and optionally delete the channel.

## Privacy

- The ticket is hidden from `@everyone` and from users outside the handling team.
- Different complaint types may be handled by different teams. Check the **Visible team** line on the confirmation page before submitting.
- Do not submit sensitive information such as private chat screenshots, doxxing information, or anything you are not comfortable sharing with that team.
- Choosing the wrong complaint type may show your report to a different team. Please choose carefully.

## Tips for a useful report

- Include the accused user's username or user ID.
- Describe what happened, where it happened, and when.
- Add message links or screenshots when possible. A link to the original message is usually more useful than a cropped screenshot.
- Keep the report factual. The handling team may ask follow-up questions in the ticket.

## Language

If you opened the English panel, the ticket and its management panel will be in English. Some administrator-only tools may still appear in Chinese; this does not affect your ability to submit and discuss your complaint.

## Need help?

If you cannot open a ticket or the panel does not respond, contact a server administrator. Do not open a public report in a normal channel.
